RemoteFM1 for Android 0.1.0

Thank you for trying RemoteFM1 on Android! This is the first Android release: the iOS app's features on Android
phones and tablets, as an APK you install yourself.

What it is: RemoteFM1 runs FM-1 firmwares on your phone or tablet. Plug in an FM-1 running the RemoteFM1 firmware and
its screen, knobs, buttons and keys become the panel for the firmware running in the app; without an FM-1 you play
the on-screen FM-1. Everything the iOS app does is here: the six built-in firmwares (ChoralRoot, Felucca, FIZZ,
Saadet, Boardwalk, LOOM-8), Get firmware for more (Melodee, JIANT and the others in the catalog), the themes, saved
states, safety copies and backups, MIDI controllers and MIDI learn, the OMX-27, the firmwares' own editors
(Firmware › Open its editor), Install RemoteFM1 on the FM-1 and Send a DX7 cartridge. States and backups move between
the iPhone and Android apps. There's no AUv3 plug-in on Android.

Install:
1. Download RemoteFM1-0.1.0.apk below on your phone or tablet.
2. Open it and allow your browser or file manager to install apps when Android asks.
3. Samsung: turn off Auto Blocker while you install (Settings › Security and privacy › Auto Blocker).
4. Later versions install over this one and keep your data.

You need Android 10 or newer on an arm64 phone or tablet. For the panel, an FM-1 with the RemoteFM1 firmware.

What I tested: everything on the Android emulator, and on my Galaxy Tab A9+ the FM-1 over USB (its screen and
buttons) and the firmwares playing through the tablet's speaker. What I couldn't test yet: Bluetooth with the FM-1,
real MIDI keyboards and the OMX-27, installing RemoteFM1 and sending cartridges to a real FM-1. If you try any of
these, I'd love to hear how it went.

Known issues:
- On some Samsung/Qualcomm devices (my Galaxy Tab A9+) sound through the FM-1's own USB audio doesn't play and can
  make the FM-1 drop off USB. It's off unless you turn on Settings › Sound through USB devices (experimental). Use the
  speaker, headphones or Bluetooth instead.

Please try

1. Plug in your FM-1 and play. Tell me your phone or tablet's model and Android version, and whether the screen,
   buttons and keys keep up.
2. Connect the FM-1 over Bluetooth. Let me know if it finds the FM-1 and how responsive it feels.
3. Play with headphones or a Bluetooth speaker and tell me if you hear clicks or dropouts (Diagnostics shows
   underruns).
